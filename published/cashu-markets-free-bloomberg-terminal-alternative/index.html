Free Bloomberg Terminal Alternative Gains Traction Among Retail Investors in 2026

Client: Cashu Markets Category: Finance Last updated: July 2026
Download PDF report ↗

Cashu Markets offers real-time data across 100,000+ stocks, ETFs, and crypto — at no cost.

Cashu Markets, a free market data terminal, is seeing growing adoption among retail investors seeking Bloomberg-caliber tools without the $24,000+ per year price tag typically charged for institutional-grade data terminals.

Professional market data platforms like Bloomberg Terminal and Refinitiv remain priced for hedge funds, banks, and institutional trading desks. Despite retail investors now accounting for a significant share of daily US equity trading volume, most self-directed traders have been left choosing between paywalled data, limited free tools, or fragmented sources spread across multiple apps and tabs.

Cashu Markets addresses that gap with a fully customizable, drag-and-drop terminal that brings together real-time prices, charts, and market data across more than 100,000 stocks, indices, forex pairs, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies — all in a single dashboard, at no cost to the user.

"Retail investors shouldn't need a Bloomberg budget to trade with institutional-grade information," said Luka, Founder at Cashu Markets. "We built Cashu Markets so that anyone can access the same caliber of real-time data that used to be locked behind a five-figure subscription."

The platform also features a Spotlight section highlighting emerging public companies, along with stock screeners covering NASDAQ and other major exchanges, giving self-directed investors research tools previously reserved for professional desks.

Cashu Markets is available now, with no signup required to access core terminal features.
